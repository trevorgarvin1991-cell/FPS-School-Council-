Schedule

Thursday, October 22, 2026. All times are PM.

  1. 3:45 PMVolunteers set up.
  2. 5:30 PMOutdoor BBQ begins by the gazebo.
  3. 6:00 PMDoors open for guests and games begin.
  4. 7:00 PMMeet in the gym to begin the hallway costume parade. Teacher surprise flash mob planned.
  5. 7:30 PMBBQ clean up begins.
  6. 7:30 PMSilent auction closes. Winners may pay and pick up items until 8:00 PM.
  7. 8:00 PMGames end and volunteers clean up.
At a glance Guest activities run from 6:00 to 8:00 PM. The BBQ is available from 5:30 to 7:30 PM, and silent auction winners should complete payment and pickup before 8:00 PM.
